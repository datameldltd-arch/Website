Questions

Before you get in touch.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we give you a clear plan and price after a short conversation. Our Starter, Growth and Premium packages are a good guide to where you might fit.

Can you work with the brand and website we already have?

Yes. We can refresh what you have, build on it, or start fresh. Tell us what is working and what is not, and we will suggest the lightest change that gets the result.

What happens if the internet goes down at the till?

Our POS systems are built offline-first, so you keep taking orders and payments flow through as normal. Everything syncs when the connection is back.

Where are you based?

We work with businesses in the UK and Pakistan. You can reach us on WhatsApp in either country, by phone, or by email.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. We train your team, and the Premium package includes ongoing support so you are not left on your own.
